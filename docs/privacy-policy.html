ClearView — Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device. Always.
Last updated: [Effective date]  ·  App: ClearView (package com.muddassir.clearview)

1. Overview

ClearView is a parental-control app that blocks adult content, user-set keywords and websites, and incognito browsing inside Chrome and the Google app. It also includes a Quran reader, a media player for YouTube content, and live-stream viewing.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no cloud sync. Everything the app does happens on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information — no name, email, phone number, address, or date of birth is requested or collected.
  • No browsing history — pages you visit are never logged or uploaded.
  • No location data — the app does not access your location.
  • No device identifiers — no advertising ID, IMEI, or similar identifiers are read or transmitted.
  • No analytics or crash reports — the app contains no tracking or analytics libraries.
  • No contact list, photos, or files — the app never accesses your contacts, media library, or documents.

3. Accessibility Service & Screen Content

ClearView uses Android's Accessibility Service to read the text of the screen currently shown in Chrome or the Google app. This is done in real time and entirely on your device, for one purpose only: to detect adult content, blocked keywords or websites, and incognito mode, so the app can block them.

  • Screen text is processed instantly in memory and is never stored.
  • Screen text is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Accessibility access can be revoked at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

Thumbnail images are screened by an on-device machine-learning model (a TFLite NSFW classifier). Images are analyzed on the device and are never uploaded.

4. Permissions We Use

  • Accessibility service — required for content blocking (see Section 3).
  • Notifications — used only to show local notifications when a saved YouTube channel uploads a new video (a feature you can turn off).
  • Internet — used only to fetch content you request: the Quran translation text, and video metadata/streams/thumbnails from YouTube.
  • Device admin — optional; used only to add uninstall protection when you enable it.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

All settings and data live in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your blocked keywords and blocked websites lists.
  • App settings (protection toggles, refresh intervals, notification preference).
  • Your Block-tab password, stored only as a SHA-256 hash (never in plain text).
  • Saved YouTube channels, video feed caches, and watch progress.
  • Quran bookmarks and the cached Quran translation text.

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it — there is no server copy, because no server exists.

6. Third-Party Services

ClearView makes standard internet requests to third parties only when you use a related feature:

  • AlQuran.Cloud — the Quran translation text is downloaded once from this public API and cached locally.
  • YouTube / Google — video metadata (RSS feeds), thumbnails, and playback via YouTube's official embedded player when you view or play videos.

As with any internet use, these services may see your IP address and standard request metadata. ClearView does not send them any account information or personal data, and it does not control their privacy practices — please review their policies as well. Playback of YouTube content is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service.

7. Children's Privacy

ClearView is a parental-control tool designed to be set up and managed by an adult. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because all processing is on-device and no data is transmitted, there is nothing to store about a child using the device.

8. Security

Your data is protected by design: it is stored only in the app's private, sandboxed storage on your device; your Block-tab password is hashed with SHA-256; and no data ever leaves your device, so there is nothing for third parties to intercept or for us to lose.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing to retain on our side. You can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your device's Settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Since ClearView currently has no data collection, future changes would only reflect new features or legal requirements.
